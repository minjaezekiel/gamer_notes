What Survives A Save

The round trip, one stage at a time. Used in: web I-L11 · python A-L3 · c++ A-L8

Saving a game is a round trip: your living object becomes text, the text is stored, and later the text becomes an object again. The trip is lossy, and the losses are silent. Nothing throws, nothing warns — the value is just quietly different when it comes back.

What to look for Press Step 1 frame four times to walk one object through the four stages. Then read the right-hand column: every row marked in red came back as something other than what went in. Notice that none of them produced an error. The method was dropped, the undefined disappeared, Infinity came back as null, and the date is now a string that looks like a date and is not one.
