Enemies That Seem To Think

A state machine with legs. Used in: web I-L10 · python I-L10 · c++ I-L11

Game enemies are not intelligent and do not need to be. A guard that walks a route, notices you, runs at you, and backs off when hurt will be described by players as “smart” — and it is three states and two numbers. The illusion comes from the switching, not from the thinking.

What to look for Drag the blue player. Cross the dashed circle and watch the state box change, not the enemy's code. Then notice the second, larger circle: once the guard has seen you it keeps chasing until you are much further away than the distance that triggered it. Try shrinking that gap to zero and walk along the edge — the guard flickers between patrol and chase several times a second. That flicker has a name, and fixing it is what the gap is for.
