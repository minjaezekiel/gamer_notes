Tile Collision, One Axis at a Time

The two-step move that makes walls solid. Used in: web I-L5 and I-L12 · python I-L6 and I-L9 · c++ I-L8

Moving a box against a wall looks like one job. It is two: move sideways and fix sideways, then move vertically and fix vertically. Programmers who do it in one go get a player who sticks to walls, slides up them, or teleports through corners — and the bug is almost impossible to read from the code.

What to look for The dashed box is where the player tried to go. The solid box is where they ended up. Step one frame at a time and watch the two separate corrections: the red fix is horizontal, the orange fix is vertical. Now switch on “move both axes at once” and walk diagonally into a corner. The player climbs the wall. Nothing in that code looks wrong, which is exactly why this is worth seeing.
