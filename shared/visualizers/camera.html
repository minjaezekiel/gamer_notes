The Camera

World space, screen space, and one subtraction. Used in: web I-L6 · python I-L7 · c++ I-L9

There is no camera. Nothing in your game moves a camera, because no such object exists. What you actually have is one number you subtract from everything before you draw it. Once that lands, scrolling worlds stop being mysterious.

What to look for Two pictures of the same moment. On the left, the whole world, where the player really is. On the right, what the player sees. The player is at world x = 1200 and screen x = 240 at the same time. Watch the dead zone: inside it the camera does not move at all, and the player drifts across the screen. That is not laziness; a camera that reacts to every tiny step makes people feel sick.
