Tweens and Easing

Reshaping time. Used in: web I-L9 · python I-L11 · c++ I-L12

Nothing in the real world starts at full speed and stops dead. A door, a thrown ball, a drawer, your own hand — all of them build up and settle down. Games copy that with easing, and easing is a much smaller idea than it sounds: you take a number that goes from 0 to 1, and you bend it.

What to look for All five dots leave at the same instant and arrive at the same instant. The journey takes exactly the same time for every one of them. Only the shape of the journey differs, and that shape is the whole of what people mean by “game feel”. Watch the top one — the straight line — and notice how machine-like it looks next to the others.
